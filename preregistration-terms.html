Pre-registration reward — Stack Hop: Tower Blocks

From Circumpolar Apps, developer of Stack Hop: Tower Blocks on Google Play (com.circumpolarapps.stackhop). Updated 19-October-2026.

Pre-register for Stack Hop on Google Play before it launches and get the Founder's Pack free when you first open the game. No purchase is needed.

1. The reward

The Founder's Pack holds:

  • Nugget, a golden chick character. It is not sold in the game. It changes how your character looks, not how the game plays.
  • 3,000 coins.
  • 3 each of the Sticky, Strong Blocks and Wider powerups.

2. Who gets it

  • Anyone who taps Pre-register on the game's Google Play listing while pre-registration is open, in a country where it is offered.
  • One reward per Google account, given to the account that pre-registered.
  • Pre-registration ends when the game is released.

3. How it arrives

  • Google Play delivers the reward. It reaches the game when you install it and open it, connected to the internet, on a phone signed in to Google Play with the account that pre-registered. The game then shows a message saying you have it.
  • If it does not arrive, open the game while online, then try Settings → Restore Purchases. If it still does not arrive, write to us (section 5).

4. Conditions

  • The reward has no cash value. It cannot be exchanged for money, sold or moved to another account.
  • Coins and powerups are game items, spent like any others. Reset Progress in Settings removes them as it removes all progress; Nugget stays.
  • If Google Play reverses the reward (for example, for an account it finds was used to abuse pre-registration), the game removes Nugget as well.
  • Google Play's own terms apply to pre-registration (play.google.com/about/play-terms).
  • We may update these terms. Changes do not take away a reward already delivered.

5. Contact

Questions about the reward: circumpolarapps@gmail.com
