Stack Hop: Tower Blocks

One tap to drop the block. Land it dead centre to keep your full width. How high can you build?

Get it on Google Play

The game

  • A campaign of 27 chapters in four acts, from windy hills and haunted nights to the deep sea, outer space and a castle under siege. Each place changes how the tower plays.
  • Three endless modes: Classic, Time Attack, and Zen, where a miss never ends the run.
  • Powerups, characters and themes to collect, including a pure-black AMOLED theme.
  • Plays offline. No account, and your progress stays on your phone.
